THE CARD STUDIO

How to Make Your Card Design

1. Create a card

Open the left menu, select Card Editor, and choose New card if you want to start from scratch. Select Attack (pink), Modifier (green), or Fate (yellow). Your chosen type controls how the card is categorized.

ATTACKPrimary round card
MODIFIERPlayed with an Attack
FATESpecial effects

2. Enter card details

Enter a title, damage, stars, up to two elements (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, or None), effect/description, and optional searchable tags. Watch the live preview as you make changes.

Example

A card with 6 damage and 4 stars displays those values in the existing stat circles. The damage value contributes to the round's attack total; stars contribute toward Fate.

3. Add or draw artwork

Use the editor's image-upload controls to choose artwork from your device, draw directly in the Paint-style drawing canvas, or open Art Gallery and choose Use on Card for an existing asset. Keep drawings within the designated canvas. The preview shows how the art will appear on the card.

Finding the controls

Card Editor → artwork upload or Paint tools → card preview. The actual editor and Billy's original template are the source of truth; no replacement art is used in this guide.

4. Review before saving

Check the title, layout, readable effect text, damage, stars, elements, and artwork. Fix anything that doesn't look right in the preview.

5. Save a local draft

Click Save to My Cards. Your draft is saved in this device's browser and is not published. Open My Cards to find and continue editing it. Use Backup JSON to save an export; browser data does not automatically sync between devices.

6. Submit for approval

When ready, choose Submit to The Deck from Card Editor. Enter your private creator code when prompted. Submission places the card in the review queue—it does not immediately publish it.

7. Find the published card

After approval, the card becomes visible in The Deck. Your local draft remains separate. If your card is missing, it may still be pending review or may have been denied. Use The Deck's search and card-type filter to browse published cards.

THE GAME

Game Intro

What is the game?

Players compete for gems by secretly choosing cards, revealing them together, and comparing damage totals. Stars can unlock Fate, and special effects can change what happens.

Card types

ATTACKPrimary card
MODIFIERPaired with Attack
FATESpecial outcomes

Set up

Separate and shuffle the three decks. Each player begins with two Attack cards and two Modifier cards. Keep your hand hidden and agree on a gem target before play.

Playing a round

  1. Choose one Attack and one Modifier in secret.
  2. Place the pair face down.
  3. Everyone flips their cards simultaneously on a count of three.
  4. Add Attack + Modifier damage, then add their stars separately.
  5. Check Fate triggers and applicable card effects.
  6. Use a six-sided die result to determine the gem prize pool.
  7. Resolve the winner, award gems, record the score, and refill hands.

Damage

Damage is the total from the two cards you played. For example, 6 Attack + 2 Modifier = 8 damage. The highest final damage generally wins the prize pool, subject to card effects.

Stars and Fate

Add your Attack and Modifier stars. At 10 or more stars, you may optionally draw a blind Fate card even if you lost the round. Example: 4 + 6 = 10 stars. Fate cards can affect damage, gems, or the result. A tie may also cause tied players to draw Fate cards.

Elements

Up to two lower card circles show Earth, Water, Fire, or Air; None may represent an unused element. Do not assume elemental bonuses or weaknesses without an explicit rule or card effect.

Winning

Keep collecting gems until someone reaches the agreed target, or the deck runs out under the game's end conditions.

In Person Games

1. Prepare the decks

Shuffle separate Attack, Modifier, and Fate decks. Deal each player two Attack and two Modifier cards. Agree on a target number of gems.

2. Choose and flip

Secretly select one Attack and one Modifier, then set them face down. Count down together and manually flip both cards at the same time as everyone else.

3. Calculate the round

Add Attack and Modifier damage, and calculate the stars separately. Check card instructions and whether Fate has been activated.

4. Roll for gems

Use the manual die spinner or a physical six-sided die to generate the round's gem prize pool.

5. Award gems and continue

After resolving effects and the winner, award the gems. Record scores in Scorekeeper, refill hands, and begin the next round.

Online Games

PLANNED — NOT YET LIVE

Create or join a room

One player will create a private game room and share a code or invite. Other players will enter that code and gather in the lobby.

Choose cards privately

Each player will see a private hand and select an Attack and Modifier. Other players won't see the choices before the reveal.

Reveal and resolve

After every player locks in their choice, the cards will flip together and the game will evaluate damage, stars, and any Fate or card effects.

Animated gem roll

The game will generate the round's prize pool automatically using a visible animated die or spinner shared by all players.

Automatic leaderboard

After each round, gems will be allocated automatically and a live leaderboard will rank players by cumulative score.
